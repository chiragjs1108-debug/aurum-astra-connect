Questions before you book

Is there a spa near me in Hennur that's open today?

Yes. Aurum Astra is open every day, including Sundays and holidays, from 10 AM to 10 PM. Call or WhatsApp to check today's slots.

Do you have female therapists?

Yes. All our spa therapists are trained female professionals. Men are welcome and are looked after in the same private, professional setting.

Which full body massage should I choose?

For relaxation, choose Swedish or Aromatherapy. For knots and stiffness, Deep Tissue. After sport, a Sports Massage. For stretching without oil, Thai. For a traditional warm-oil massage, Ayurvedic Abhyanga or Balinese. We're happy to help you choose on the phone.

What should I wear or bring?

Come in whatever you're comfortable in. We provide robes and disposables, and every room has its own hot and cold shower.

Do you offer couple's massage and jacuzzi?

Yes. We have couple's suites for side-by-side massage, and a private jacuzzi and hydro jet bath. Please book a day or two ahead for couple's sessions.

Do you have any spa offers in Hennur?

First-time guests get 10% off their first therapy. Just mention this page when you call or message. We also have gift vouchers and a Prepaid Wallet for regulars.

Is parking available?

Yes, at AM Plaza, with a lift straight up to the spa on the third floor.

How do I pay?

UPI, all major credit and debit cards, or cash. All our prices include taxes.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Of course. Please give us 3 to 4 hours' notice. There's no fee.

Is there an age limit?

Spa therapies are for guests aged 18 and over.
